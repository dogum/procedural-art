The image should look like an oil painting of a small boat on a beach at sunset.

PASS if a boat shape is recognisable on a shore, the sky has warm sunset colour, and visible brush strokes or paint texture make it read as painted rather than as flat vector shapes or a photo. Loose drawing is acceptable for a draft. FAIL if no boat is recognisable, or the image is blank, a flat gradient, or broken.
