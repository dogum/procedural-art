The image should be a very wide (4:1) woodcut-style print of the Teton Range: solid black mountain masses with carved light lines, on a light background, with a large title reading TETONS.

PASS if it shows a jagged range (several sharp peaks, not one cone) in that woodcut look and the title is legible. FAIL otherwise.
