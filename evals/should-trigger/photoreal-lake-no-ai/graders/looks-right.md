The image should be a small photographic-style render of a snow-capped mountain with a lake in the foreground that reflects it.

PASS if a snowy peak is visible and the lower part of the image is water showing a mirrored mountain. FAIL if there is no water, no reflection, or the image is mostly black or broken.
