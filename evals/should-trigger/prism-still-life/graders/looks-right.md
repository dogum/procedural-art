The image should be a path-traced still life: a clear glass prism on a light surface with sunlight passing through it.

PASS if a prism is visible and there is a coloured spectrum (rainbow band) cast on the surface. Grain is acceptable for a low-sample draft. FAIL if no spectrum is visible or the image is black or broken.
